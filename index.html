REPORT PAGE 01

Star Schema & Data Model

factSales stores sales transactions, quantities, prices, order dates and foreign keys. dimProduct contains product names, brands and categories; dimStore provides store and location details; dimCustomer contains customer demographics. Each dimension connects to factSales through a many-to-one relationship.
