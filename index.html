AI Community & Sentiment Analysis

Analyzed Reddit discussions from r/ChatGPT and r/Futurology to understand how different communities use and perceive AI. Our team cleaned and combined data in BigQuery, then built Tableau visualizations comparing discussion trends, category volume, and sentiment across school, work, daily life, tasks, and concerns.

Key takeaway: AI conversations varied substantially by context, with workforce discussion especially active and concern-focused posts showing stronger negative sentiment.

PythonPandasMatplotlibREST API

Nutrition, Price & Healthy Food Marketing

Analyzed Whole Foods product data to investigate whether foods marketed as healthy provide strong nutritional value for their price. I combined pricing, nutrition, and marketing-claim datasets, integrated USDA FoodData Central data, and engineered measures including protein per dollar and sugar per dollar.

Key takeaway: Marketing language alone did not always reflect the strongest nutritional value, making price-adjusted nutrition metrics useful for comparison.
